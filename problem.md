# Asteroid Miner

## Problem

People who spend many hours working in the terminal need a quick, accessible way
to take a break without leaving it. Most games need an installation, an account or
an internet connection, and many take too long to play. They need a simple game that
is short to play and runs right where they already work.

## Initial requirements

- R1: The game runs in the terminal and needs no installation, account or internet connection; only Node.js.
- R2: The player moves a ship one square per turn on a grid with the keys W, A, S and D.
- R3: The player collects as many minerals as possible on the way to a goal square; reaching the goal ends the game.
- R4: Each move uses one unit of fuel. If the fuel runs out before the player reaches the goal, the game is lost.
- R5: After every move the screen shows the grid, the score, the fuel left and the turn number.
- R6: When the game ends, it shows a summary of the final score and everything the player collected.
